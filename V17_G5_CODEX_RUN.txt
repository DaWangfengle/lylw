SCIENCE FIRST
Experiment G5-A tests a different primitive than G4: identical frozen V17
predictive Student-t distribution, but different point decisions for 2D
Euclidean MAE. Compare ORIGINAL exact mixture mean, weighted component-centre
median (secondary control), and full continuous-mixture Monte Carlo spatial
median (primary). Student-t sampling / spatial-median algorithms ALREADY
EXIST in V9. This is hypothesis testing, NOT a new theorem or Q1 novelty.

Only Tampere TRAINING files: data/Tampere/Training_rss.csv and
data/Tampere/Training_coordinates.csv. NEVER read Test_rss.csv,
Test_coordinates.csv, UTS_test.csv, UJI official/outer, TUJI test, or
G3/G4 labelled outcome artifacts for developing or choosing this method.
This is a new physical-coordinate-disjoint 75% FIT / 25% CONFIRM internal
training-file split, deterministic SHA256 stratified by floor. All scans at
a physical coordinate stay together. Validation/confirmation scores are
one-shot, and hyperparameters are fixed before outcomes.

5 V17 R0 members: seeds 17301..17305, epochs 65/69/56/67/62; train
original architecture, frozen loss, optimizer and augmentations on FIT only.
Use exact same E5/L192/K192/alpha=1 Student-t df4 mixture for every point
action. Baseline action is the exact mixture mean. Component-centre spatial
median ignores component scale; full predictive median uses TWO 128-draw
fixed Student-t samples per query with existing V9 Weiszfeld algorithm.
Independent 2x128 fresh draws estimate predicted Euclidean Bayes risk,
without query labels. No model or density changes. ES is IDENTICAL between
actions by definition; compute only one original V17 ES diagnostic.

PROMISING_ACTION_ALIGNMENT requires BOTH: continuous-median minus mean
coordinate macro MAE <= -0.15m and familywise simultaneous 95% maxT
MAE upper <0. Bootstrap 5000x paired physical coordinate groups, one
two-contrast family: continuous median and component-centre median each
versus baseline. ES has no change; never put ES in the action-only gate.
Other outcomes must be reported, not tuned after confirmation. Spatial
dependence within floor may reduce effective sample count.
