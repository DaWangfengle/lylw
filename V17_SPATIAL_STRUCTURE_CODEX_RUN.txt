   mu_v=(mu_i+mu_j)/2;
   var_v=(var_i+var_j)/2 + gamma_space/2.

- O: original neighbor targets and original loss.

- C: replace the old unmasked NLL (do NOT add another NLL): L_C=L_A-L_nll_unmasked+1.0*L_joint_BF_XY. The joint NLL denominator includes all anchors, numerator density only true-BF anchors. All other loss terms and weights are unchanged, including 0.25*L_coord.
