======================================================================
2. HIERARCHICAL CONSENSUS LAW
======================================================================

V18.3 has no learned gate.

Within-family source weights are deterministic functions of normalized source
entropy and are hard-bounded to [0.30, 0.70].

Family reliability depends on:

- family normalized entropy;
- mean Jensen-Shannon disagreement with the other two families;
- for the Radio family only, an additional radio mismatch penalty based on
  marked-radio energy and affine style correction.

Family weights are:

w_f = m + (1 - 3m) softmax(r_f / tau)

with m=0.15.

This guarantees every family positive mass.

During training only:

- with probability 0.30 per scan, exactly one family is dropped;
- at most one family can be dropped;
- the remaining two family weights are renormalized.

At inference:

- family dropout is disabled.

Global family disagreement creates model-internal softening:

T_dis(x) =
  clip(1 + global_JS_disagreement(x), 1, 2).

The consensus posterior is softened by T_dis before final K=64 contraction.
