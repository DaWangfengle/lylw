The probe compares exactly four point decisions from the SAME frozen posterior:

2. map_component

No fifth decoder may be added after seeing results.

Therefore:

- the probe must NOT report alternative NLL or Energy Score as if each decoder
  were a different probabilistic model.

=================================================
6. PREREGISTERED DECISION RULE
=================================================

The reference is posterior_mean.

A mechanism passes only if its coordinate-macro MAE improves by at least 10%
relative to posterior_mean on BOTH:

- early_stop
- calibration

and does not worsen either role by more than 0.25 m.

Decision logic:

A. geometric_median passes:
   conclusion =
   loss_aligned_geometric_median_supported

   V18.4 may register weighted geometric median as its formal point decoder.

B. geometric_median fails but top8_mean passes:
   conclusion =
   posterior_tail_dilution_supported

   V18.4 should treat trust-region posterior contraction/transport as the
   primary fix. Do not promote geometric median from this evidence alone.

C. neither passes:
   conclusion =
   posterior_learning_dominates

   V18.4 should prioritize trust-region posterior inference plus spatial OT.
   Decoder mismatch must not be claimed as a primary contribution.

Do not change the 10% threshold or 0.25 m rule after results are observed.
